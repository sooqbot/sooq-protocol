# SOOQ Whitepaper

## Vision

A market floor anyone can read at a glance. Solana moves fast and loudly; SOOQ turns the noise of promoted pairs into one ranked board that tells you where the traffic really is.

## Mission

Show what is trading, how deep the pool runs and how far a day's quote swung, using public sources only, and say plainly when a fact has not been stated.

## Principles

- One register holds every outside fact. A fact that is not stated stays visible, quiet and unlinked.
- The board depends on the market feed record and on nothing else.
- Motion serves reading. Everything can be stilled.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: The floor

Ship the live ledger, ticker, summary tiles and lenses on a static site.

### Phase 2: The register

Fill the register as facts arrive: mint, social links, repository. Each record turns live without a layout change.

### Phase 3: Depth

Add per-stall detail panels and longer reads of pool depth, still from public endpoints.

### Phase 4: Community

Open the repository, publish contribution notes and invite stall curators.
